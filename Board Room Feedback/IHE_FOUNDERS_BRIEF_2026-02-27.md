## 🔥 THE PROBLEM — WHAT PAIN DOES THIS SOLVE?

Higher education professionals face an acute challenge: AI is transforming every aspect of their work (teaching, assessment, administration, student services) but most institutions provide inadequate guidance, training, or resources.

**Specific pain points:**
1. **Information overload without curation** — Hundreds of AI tools launch monthly. Faculty don't know which ones are trustworthy, classroom-tested, or relevant to higher ed
2. **No daily briefing for their world** — Inside Higher Ed and EdSurge cover news broadly, but there's no focused daily AI briefing designed specifically for higher ed practitioners
3. **Generic AI advice doesn't translate** — Most AI content targets business or K-12. Higher ed has unique challenges: academic integrity, tenure politics, shared governance, adjunct faculty constraints, accreditation requirements
4. **Isolation** — Innovators at their institutions often feel alone. They need a community of practice
5. **Skills gap without training pipeline** — Faculty need prompt engineering skills, AI literacy, and pedagogical frameworks for AI-enhanced teaching, but PD budgets are thin

**Market context:**
- AI usage in higher ed surged 2.3× in one year
- 93% of faculty/administrators expect to expand AI use in next two years
- Only ~33% of campus CIOs feel their institution is prepared for AI growth
- 74% of faculty cite process complexity as barrier to innovation adoption

## 🏁 WHY NOW?

1. **AI adoption inflection point** — We're past the hype cycle and into the "now what do we actually DO with it" phase. Faculty need practical guidance more than ever.
2. **Competitor gap** — No one owns the "daily AI briefing for higher ed" category. Inside Higher Ed is broad news. Enrollify focuses on enrollment marketing. Teaching in Higher Ed is broad pedagogy. EdSurge is K-12 heavy. The intersection of AI + higher ed + daily practical content is unclaimed.
3. **Website refresh timing** — Moving from WordPress to a modern Next.js/Vercel stack gives IHE the technical foundation to scale features (dynamic content, API-driven directories, interactive prompts) that WordPress couldn't support.
4. **Portfolio synergy** — IHE becomes the top-of-funnel content engine feeding qualified leads to the AI OER Platform, AI College Rankings cross-links, and consulting/workshop services.

---

## 🛡️ UNFAIR ADVANTAGE

1. **Dr. Norma Jones is the product.** She's not just a host — she's an active faculty member, AI trainer, and recognized leader in California AI education initiatives. She has credibility that content-only platforms can't replicate. Faculty trust faculty.
2. **Already operating.** IHE isn't a concept — it has 9+ podcast episodes, 39+ reviewed tools, 24+ prompt templates, 1,200+ newsletter subscribers, and a daily briefing running. This is an optimization problem, not a validation problem.
3. **The Jones portfolio creates a moat.** IHE (content/community) + AI College Rankings (diagnostic pressure) + AI OER Platform (product solution) + Vibe Presenter (classroom delivery) = an integrated ecosystem that no single competitor replicates.
4. **Community college access.** Dr. Jones's network at AVC and across the California CC system provides direct access to the largest segment of higher ed (community colleges enroll ~40% of all U.S. undergraduates) that most edtech platforms ignore.
