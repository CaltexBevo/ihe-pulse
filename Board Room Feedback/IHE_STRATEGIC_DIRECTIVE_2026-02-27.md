## The Board's Strategic Directives

### Directive 1: The Innovation Pulse Is the Product

The Innovation Pulse — the daily curated AI briefing — is not a feature of the website. It IS the business. Everything else (podcast, AI Directory, Prompt Navigator, Tinker Lab) is supporting content that feeds subscribers into the Pulse.

**The name "The Innovation Pulse" is approved.** It does not need "Higher Ed" in the name because it lives under the InnovatingHigherEd.com umbrella. The parent brand provides context. This is clean, memorable, and already on the site.

**Do NOT use "IHE" as a brand abbreviation.** Inside Higher Ed owns "IHE" in the higher education space. Using it creates brand confusion. Always use "Innovating Higher Ed" or "The Innovation Pulse" — never abbreviate to IHE in external-facing content.

### Directive 2: Design for Community Colleges, Brand for Everyone

Community colleges represent the largest segment of U.S. higher education (~1,000+ institutions, ~40% of all undergraduates). Dr. Jones's network, expertise, and credibility are strongest here. The Board recommends:

**Target CC faculty in execution, not in branding.** The positioning stays broad: "the daily AI briefing built by educators, for educators." But the content should consistently reflect community college realities:
- Limited budgets and no AI support staff
- Heavy teaching loads (4-5 courses per semester)
- Adjunct-heavy departments with minimal PD budgets
- Students who are working adults, first-generation, diverse
- Practical, immediate classroom applications over theoretical research

This approach makes CC faculty feel seen without putting a limiting label on the brand. A state university dean reading the same content will find it equally relevant — the pain points aren't exclusive to CCs, they're just most acute there.

### Directive 4: Operational Sustainability

The HOO (Head of Operations) flagged this as the #1 risk. Two people, one of whom is full-time faculty, running a daily content operation plus four other products.

**Portfolio priority stack:**
1. **Innovating Higher Ed** — The audience engine. Without this, nothing else has distribution.
2. **AI OER Platform** — Highest revenue potential. Pre-build, can be paced.
3. **AI College Rankings** — Largely built, minimal maintenance.
4. **InnerYoo** — MVP near complete, maintenance mode.
5. **Vibe Presenter** — Longest timeline, can wait.

**Content cadence reality check:** Daily Innovation Pulse + weekly podcast + directory maintenance is ambitious for two people. The Board recommends:
- Batch-produce 3-5 Pulse editions on weekends
- Use AI-assisted drafting (RSS feeds + AI summary → human editorial review)
- Reduce Tinker Lab to monthly
- Merge Educator Tools into AI Directory
