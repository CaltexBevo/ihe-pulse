YOUR AI JOURNEY

Getting Started with AI

A 10-step path to AI fluency for higher education professionals.

1

Understand What AI Actually Is

Start with the fundamentals. Learn the difference between generative AI, machine learning, and traditional software. Demystify the technology before diving in.

2

Have Your First AI Conversation

Open ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini and simply start talking. Ask about your research, explore a topic for your next lecture, or get help drafting an email.

3

Learn Basic Prompt Engineering

Discover how to get better responses through role prompting, providing context, and being specific about your desired output format.

4

Find Your First Quick Win

Identify one repetitive task in your workflow — drafting assignment instructions, creating discussion questions, summarizing readings — and let AI help.

5

Explore the Tools Directory

Browse our curated collection of 200+ AI tools specifically vetted for higher education. Find tools for your discipline and use case.

6

Understand AI Policy Landscape

Review your institution's AI policy (or help create one). Understand the ethical considerations around privacy, bias, and academic integrity.

7

Design an AI-Inclusive Assignment

Create an assignment that intentionally incorporates AI as a learning partner. Focus on higher-order thinking that AI enhances rather than replaces.

8

Listen to the Podcast

Hear from educators who are already innovating with AI. Their stories provide practical insights and inspiration for your own journey.

9

Join the Community

Connect with 200+ educators exploring AI in higher ed. Share experiences, ask questions, and learn from peers across institutions.

10

Share & Teach Others

The best way to learn is to teach. Present at a faculty meeting, write about your experiences, or mentor a colleague just starting out.

Dr. Norma Jones

AI Integration Leader & Educator

Dr. Norma Jones is uniquely positioned to guide conversations about the future of higher education and artificial intelligence. With extensive experience as a presenter, author, editor, keynote speaker, and communication expert, Norma effortlessly bridges academia and executive leadership.

Her career spans diverse sectors including education, energy, law enforcement, international business, fine jewelry, eCommerce, multimedia entertainment, and advertising—giving her a distinctive ability to understand and connect complex ideas across industries.

A recognized leader in AI initiatives throughout California, Norma has empowered countless educators and students by facilitating meaningful integration of AI into classrooms and institutional strategy. Her expertise in communication and faculty development fosters inclusive, technology-enhanced learning environments designed to elevate student success.

As host of Innovating Higher Ed, Norma's insightful perspective, dynamic speaking style, and deep understanding of both academic and corporate innovation make her the perfect guide for exploring new frontiers in educational transformation.

Whether you're looking to enhance classroom experiences, streamline operations, or stay ahead of the latest edtech trends, Innovating Higher Ed is your trusted partner in embracing the future of education.

The Wonka-Lantern Framework: Creative & Ethical AI

June 17, 2025 • Dr. Norma Jones

Audio Version
12:30
🍎 Apple 🎵 Spotify 🎧 Audible 📦 Podbean

What if we approached AI integration with the creative wonder of Willy Wonka and the principled wisdom of Diogenes? The Wonka-Lantern Framework offers a new mental model for educators navigating AI adoption.

The Two Poles

The Wonka Mindset: Creative experimentation, playful exploration, "what if we tried..." thinking. This pole encourages educators to dream big, test wild ideas, and embrace the magical potential of AI tools.

The Lantern Principle: Ethical illumination, principled boundaries, shining light on consequences. Like Diogenes with his lantern searching for an honest person, this pole asks us to examine our practices critically.

Practical Application

When evaluating any AI integration, ask both questions:

  • Wonka: What creative possibilities does this unlock?
  • Lantern: What ethical considerations must we address?

Neither pole alone is sufficient. Pure creativity without ethics risks harm. Pure caution without creativity risks stagnation. The framework helps educators find the dynamic balance.

Framework in Practice

Try this exercise with your next AI tool adoption decision: Create two columns. In the Wonka column, list all the creative, exciting possibilities. In the Lantern column, list all the ethical questions and concerns. Then look for where the columns intersect — that's your sweet spot for responsible innovation.

ChatGPT Pro Deep Research: Worth It?

February 28, 2025 • Dr. Norma Jones

Audio Version
15:45
🍎 Apple 🎵 Spotify 🎧 Audible 📦 Podbean

OpenAI's ChatGPT Pro tier promises "Deep Research" capabilities for $200/month. As a higher education professional, is it worth the premium? I spent a month putting it through its paces.

What Deep Research Does

Deep Research is an autonomous research mode where ChatGPT browses the web, synthesizes sources, and produces comprehensive reports. It can spend 5-30 minutes working through complex research questions.

My Testing Methodology

I tested Deep Research on tasks relevant to higher ed professionals:

  • Literature review on AI in community colleges
  • Policy comparison across institutions
  • Grant landscape analysis
  • Curriculum alignment research

The Verdict

Strengths: Impressive synthesis, good source diversity, substantial time savings on exploratory research.

Limitations: Cannot access paywalled academic databases, occasional hallucinated citations, expensive for individual users.

Bottom Line: Worth it for administrators and researchers who frequently need broad landscape analysis. Less essential for classroom instructors. Consider institutional licensing to spread costs.
