💡 My Recommendation

🟣 Plum creates the strongest visual distinction while harmonizing with both orange and blue. It adds warmth that connects to your orange, while the purple undertones complement your blue. The soft teal is fresh but may feel too similar to your existing blue. The navy is subtle but might get lost against the dark background.
