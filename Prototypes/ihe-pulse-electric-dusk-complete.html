Dr. Norma Jones

Founder & Host of Innovating Higher Ed

Dr. Norma Jones is a recognized leader in AI integration for higher education, with over two decades of experience in faculty development and educational technology. Her work focuses on helping educators confidently navigate the rapidly evolving landscape of AI-driven teaching and learning.

Through the Innovating Higher Ed podcast and platform, Dr. Jones shares real-world examples, practical strategies, and conversations with thought leaders who are transforming education through innovation.

Our Mission

We're committed to empowering higher education professionals with the knowledge, tools, and community they need to harness AI's transformative potential—ethically, effectively, and in service of student success.
