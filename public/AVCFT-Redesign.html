Know Your Rights

Before any meeting, before you sign anything — read this

Your first step in almost every situation is the same: contact AVCFT before responding, signing, or attending any meeting alone. Email contact@avcft.org or call (661) 450-8437.

Weingarten Rights — say this before the meeting begins
"I request union representation before this meeting continues."

You have the right to union representation at any investigatory meeting that could lead to discipline. Management cannot penalize you for invoking it. If representation is refused: document the refusal, withdraw from the meeting, and contact AVCFT immediately.

Deadline

The 20-day rule

You must begin informal resolution within 20 instructional days of learning about a violation — instructional days, not calendar days. Missing the window permanently waives the grievance. When in doubt, contact AVCFT the same week.

Probationary faculty

Non-reemployment notice

Notice must arrive by certified or registered mail (Art. VIII §4.4.8(p)). If notice was never properly received, the clock never started — a mandatory remedy may apply. Contact AVCFT immediately.

Discipline

Progressive discipline & just cause

Verbal warning → written reprimand → suspension → termination, in that order, and all of it requires just cause (Art. VII §2.5). Skipped steps are grievable.

Workload

The caps that protect you

25 LHE full-time cap per term (Art. X §3.5.1) — overload requires your consent. Involuntary reduction triggers 10-calendar-day bumping rights (§14.10). The 50% Rule (Art. XVII §4.0) limits distance-ed changes without negotiation.

Who to contact, when

Reprimand, investigation, or non-reemployment notice

Contact the President immediately. Do not respond in writing or attend any meeting before consulting AVCFT. Invoke Weingarten rights at any meeting.

Possible contract violation

The informal resolution window is 20 instructional days. Contact the President or the Grievance Officer quickly, with dates and specifics documented.
