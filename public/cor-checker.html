COR §55001.5(b) Checker

Upload a Course Outline of Record to check compliance with California's IDEAA requirements across all relevant COR sections.

⚠ Audit Risk §55001.5(b) requires CORs to include approaches for engaging diverse students, advancing equitable outcomes, and promoting inclusion. This is currently on an honor system basis but colleges may be audited at any time. The COR is a legally binding document that drives teaching strategies, guides all instructors, affects transfer articulation, and is visible to students. If your college has adopted CCN templates, IDEAA language is built into Part 1 — but Part 2 (descriptions, objectives, content, methods) is customizable and must also reflect IDEAA principles.
