Faculty judgment required. This is a transparent textual evidence finder, not an AI semantic evaluator or an institutional compliance determination. A high score means useful words and phrases appear together, not that depth, accuracy, accessibility, assignments, or learning outcomes are fully satisfied. “No clear match” does not prove content is absent. Course delivery and assessment requirements must be reviewed separately.

Made for curious educators, meticulous curriculum folks, and anyone who's ever wondered whether a square textbook belongs in a round syllabus. PDF text extraction requires an internet connection to load the PDF reader library on first use; analysis runs in this browser.
